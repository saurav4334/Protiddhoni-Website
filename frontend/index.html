Python 
# Install: pip install voicereach
import voicereach

# Initialize client
client = voicereach.Client("your_api_key_here")

# Send a voice broadcast
response = client.broadcasts.create(
    audio_url="https://your-audio.mp3",
    contacts=["+8801711111111", "+8801822222222"],
    caller_id="VOICEREACH",
    schedule=None
)

# Check delivery status
print(f"Broadcast ID: {response.id}")
print(f"Status: {response.status}")
print(f"Reach: {response.reach_count}")

Questions, answered

How fast can I launch my first broadcast?+

Under 3 minutes. Sign up, upload a contacts CSV, record a 15-second audio, and hit send — we handle the rest.

Which carriers do you support?+

All four major Bangladeshi carriers: Grameenphone, Robi, Banglalink, and Airtel — plus landlines via BTCL.

How is pricing calculated?+

One pulse = 30 seconds of successful call. You only pay for answered calls. Free retries. Starter ৳0.40/pulse, Growth ৳0.35/pulse.

Is Voice OTP really more secure than SMS?+

Yes. Voice OTP is immune to SIM swap attacks, SS7 interception, and message forwarding — three vectors that plague SMS OTP globally.

Absolutely. Every new account gets 15 free voice credits on signup — no credit card required.
