Voice OTP vs SMS OTP: Which One Is More Secure for Businesses?

📅 Feb 10, 2026⏱ 6 min read
🔐

One-time passwords (OTPs) are the backbone of modern authentication — from banking logins to e-commerce checkouts. Most businesses default to SMS OTP, but a growing number are switching to voice OTP (an automated call that reads the code aloud) for higher delivery and stronger fraud resistance.

What is Voice OTP?

Voice OTP places an automated phone call to the customer and speaks the verification code in clear audio — in Bangla, English, or both. Because it travels over the voice network rather than the SMS gateway, it reaches users even when messaging is congested, filtered, or blocked.

What is SMS OTP?

SMS OTP sends the code as a text message through an SMS aggregator and carrier route. It is simple and familiar, but its delivery depends on gateway quality, sender-ID rules, and carrier filtering — all of which vary widely across regions.

Security comparison

  • Interception: SMS can be exposed to SIM-swap and SS7 attacks; a spoken code delivered to the registered number is harder to silently intercept.
  • Delivery proof: Voice calls return real-time answered/failed signals, so you know if the customer actually received the code.
  • Spoofing & phishing: A branded outbound call is harder to mimic than a forwarded text.
  • Liveness: A call requires the user to answer their own device at that moment.

Deliverability in Bangladesh

In markets like Bangladesh, SMS open and delivery rates drop on congested or rural networks. Voice OTP consistently lands across all four carriers because a ringing phone gets answered. For time-sensitive flows — bank transfers, account recovery, high-value checkouts — that reliability directly protects conversion and trust.

When to use which

Use SMS OTP for low-risk, high-volume, cost-sensitive flows. Use Voice OTP as the primary channel for high-value transactions, or as an automatic fallback when SMS is not delivered within a few seconds. Many teams combine both: try SMS first, then escalate to a voice call.

Bottom line

Voice OTP is not a replacement for every SMS — it is a more reliable, harder-to-intercept layer for the moments that matter most. For businesses where a missed OTP means a lost customer or a security risk, voice verification is the safer default.

Frequently asked questions

Is voice OTP more secure than SMS OTP?

For high-value flows, yes — voice OTP is more resistant to SIM-swap and SS7 interception and gives real-time delivery confirmation, while SMS depends on gateway and carrier filtering.

Does voice OTP work on all Bangladesh carriers?

Yes. Protiddhoni delivers voice OTP across Grameenphone, Robi, Banglalink, and Airtel with automatic routing for the best delivery rate.

Can I use voice OTP as a fallback to SMS?

Absolutely. A common setup is SMS-first, then an automatic voice call if the SMS is not delivered within a few seconds — maximising delivery without extra cost on successful texts.
