Voice Broadcast

What Is Voice Broadcasting? A Complete Guide for Businesses

📅 Feb 12, 2026⏱ 7 min read
📢

Voice broadcasting (also called bulk voice calling) sends a single recorded or AI-generated voice message to many phone numbers simultaneously. It is one of the fastest, most personal ways to reach a large audience — no internet or app required on the recipient’s side.

How voice broadcasting works

  1. Upload your contact list and a message (record audio or generate it with text-to-speech).
  2. The platform queues and dials numbers across carriers with smart routing.
  3. When a call is answered, the message plays; recipients can press a key (DTMF) to act.
  4. You get live delivery, answer, and response analytics.

Common use cases

  • Alerts & notifications — payment due, delivery updates, service outages.
  • Promotions — time-sensitive offers and event reminders.
  • Public outreach — health, education, and government announcements.
  • Reminders — appointments, renewals, and follow-ups.

Why businesses choose voice over SMS or email

Voice cuts through inbox clutter and SMS filtering. A ringing phone commands attention, the message is heard in the customer’s own language, and personalised audio builds trust. For urgent or high-value communication, answer rates are typically far higher than text.

Best practices

  • Keep messages under 30 seconds and lead with the key point.
  • Use Bangla TTS or a natural recording for local audiences.
  • Respect calling hours and DND rules; include an opt-out.
  • Add a clear call to action — “press 1 to confirm”.
  • Test on a small segment before the full broadcast.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between voice broadcasting and a robocall?

They use the same technology, but voice broadcasting refers to legitimate, opt-in business messaging — alerts, reminders, and announcements — sent with consent and an opt-out, unlike spam robocalls.

How many calls can I send at once?

Campaigns scale from a few hundred to lakhs of numbers. Protiddhoni queues and routes calls across all BD carriers so large broadcasts complete quickly.

Can recipients respond to a broadcast?

Yes — using keypad (DTMF) input, recipients can press a key to confirm, opt out, or be routed to an agent, and every response is captured in analytics.
