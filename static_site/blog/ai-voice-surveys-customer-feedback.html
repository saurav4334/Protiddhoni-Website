How AI Voice Surveys Improve Customer Feedback Collection

📅 Feb 14, 2026⏱ 6 min read
📊

Collecting honest customer feedback is hard: email surveys go unopened and web forms get abandoned. AI voice surveys solve this by calling customers, asking questions in a natural voice, and recording answers automatically — no app, no link, no typing.

What is an AI voice survey?

An AI voice survey is an automated phone call that asks a series of questions and collects responses through keypad input (DTMF) or spoken answers. AI handles branching logic, transcription, and analysis, turning thousands of calls into structured data.

How AI improves the process

  • Natural conversation: Bangla and English voices that sound human, with dynamic follow-up questions.
  • Auto-transcription: Spoken feedback is converted to text and themes automatically.
  • Smart routing: Detractors can be escalated to a human in real time.
  • Scale: Run the same survey across districts in hours, not weeks.

DTMF vs speech capture

Use DTMF (“press 1 for yes”) for fast, unambiguous ratings like NPS and CSAT. Use speech capture for open-ended comments where you want the customer’s own words. Most surveys combine both.

Where voice surveys shine

  • Post-purchase and post-delivery satisfaction (CSAT)
  • Net Promoter Score (NPS) tracking
  • Market research across rural and urban areas
  • Service quality and support follow-ups

The payoff

Because a call reaches people who never open a form, voice surveys consistently achieve higher completion rates and a more representative sample — giving you feedback you can actually trust.

Frequently asked questions

How do customers answer an AI voice survey?

They respond using their phone keypad (DTMF) for rating questions, or by speaking for open-ended ones. AI transcribes and analyses the answers automatically.

Are voice surveys better than online forms?

For reach, yes. Voice surveys connect with people who never open email or web links, so completion rates and sample representativeness are typically much higher.

Can I run a voice survey in Bangla?

Yes. Protiddhoni supports natural Bangla and English voices, and open-ended Bangla responses are auto-transcribed for analysis.
