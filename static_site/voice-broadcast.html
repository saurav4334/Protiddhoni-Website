What is Voice Broadcast?

Voice Broadcast lets your business send a single pre-recorded or AI-generated voice message to many phone numbers automatically. Instead of dialing customers one by one, you upload your contact list, choose your message, and the system delivers the call for you — making it a simple way to communicate with a large audience at the same time.
