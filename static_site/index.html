Frequently asked

Common questions, straight answers.

What's the difference between Voice OTP and SMS OTP?

SMS often fails in rural areas due to network congestion and low priority queues. A voice call rings directly on the user's phone — even with poor data. Voice OTP delivery rate is 99.4%, while SMS only achieves 78–85% for similar campaigns. Voice is also harder to fake — better fraud prevention.

How is the Bangla voice quality? Will it sound robotic?

Our Bangla TTS engine is trained with local linguists, with natural prosody and regional accent support (Dhaka, Sylhet, Chittagong). Both female and male voices are available. You can also record and upload your own voice — pre-recorded messages sound 100% natural.

Which carriers do you support?

All 4 BD carriers: Grameenphone, Robi, Banglalink, Airtel. Plus international roaming numbers (+880 prefix outside BD). Auto-routing — just provide the number and we detect the carrier and pick the optimal route.

How does pricing work?

Per-call pricing with monthly plan. Starter (৳999/mo) e 1,000 calls included. Beyond that, ৳0.85-1.20 per call depending on plan. No hidden fees, no carrier surcharges. Volume discount available for 50K+ calls/month.

Compliance and regulations? Do you have BTRC permission?

Yes — fully BTRC compliant. DND (Do Not Disturb) registry integration, opt-in consent verification, and complete audit logs. ISO 27001 + PCI-DSS compliance documents are available for banking and government clients.

Integration koto somoy lage?

Voice OTP basic integration: 5-15 minutes (3 lines of code). Full broadcast platform with custom IVR flows: typically 1-3 days with our SDK. Pre-built Zapier, WordPress, Shopify integrations are zero-code.

What's included in the free trial?

50 free voice OTP calls + full dashboard access for 14 days. No credit card needed. Test your integration, see real delivery analytics, then upgrade when ready.

Are failed calls charged?

No — only successful connected calls count. Calls that hit busy, switched-off, or unreachable numbers are free. You only pay for actually connected calls.
