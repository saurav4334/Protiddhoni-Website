Voice Survey FAQ

Questions about running surveys

How do voice surveys get higher response rates?

SMS reply rate is ~12%, online-survey link click rate is ~3%. A voice call rings directly, the customer picks up, and a 1–5 button press takes 30 seconds. Result: 60–70% completion rate. Far more natural for Bangla speakers.

Can respondents speak open-ended answers?

Yes — every question can capture either DTMF (button press) or a voice recording (10–60 seconds). Open-ended Bangla recordings are auto-transcribed and sentiment-tagged automatically.

What about data privacy?

Respondent consent recorded automatically (intro message e). All data stored in BD-resident servers, encrypted. PII can be auto-redacted from transcripts. GDPR-compliant + Bangladesh DPDP-ready.

Can I target specific districts or demographics?

Definitely — you can filter your uploaded list by district, division, age range (if known), or gender. Or set quotas: "stop after 200 responses from each district" for representative samples.

What if a respondent is busy?

Configurable retry — by default we'll try 3 times spread across 24h. Voicemail detection avoids burning your budget. Respondent can also press 9 to "call back later" and we'll auto-reschedule.

How fast can I see results?

Real-time. Dashboard updates every second as calls complete. NPS, completion %, district breakdown — all live. CSV export available as soon as responses come in.

Can I integrate with my CRM?

Yes — native integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Pipedrive, plus webhooks for any custom CRM. Survey trigger from CRM events ("close ticket → trigger CSAT survey 24h later") supported.
