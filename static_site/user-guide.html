Step 1

Login & dashboard ke chinun

Browser-e tomar admin URL kholo (jemon: protiddhoni-bd.com/admin). Email + password diyo, ar 2FA code (Google Authenticator app theke).

📍 Keno 2FA must?

Super Admin role-er jonno 2FA force kora ache. Keu password churi korleo tomar phone chhara dhukte parbe na.

Login holey **Dashboard**-e land korbe. Eikhane chokher poloke 4 Ta KPI, 4-5 Ta chart, ar latest activity feed dekhte pabe — site er pulse ektai screen-e.

💡 Pro tip

Top-bar e ⌘K (Mac) ba Ctrl+K (Windows) press korle quick search khulbe — kothao theke tumi posts, users, media e jump korte parbe.

Step 2 — most common workflow

Ekta blog post publish koro

Real example: tumi likhte chao "Eid Sale offer 2026" niye ekTa blog post. Niche full flow — start to publish — 6 substep e bhanga.

2a Editor khulun

Sidebar e Content → New Post click koro, ba Dashboard-er top bar e + New button click koro. Ekdom blank ekTa editor khulbe.

  • Title field-e likho: "Eid Sale 2026: How to launch a voice campaign in 2 hours"
  • Niche **URL slug** auto-generate hobe — chaile manually edit korte parbe
  • Title likhar shathe shathe **draft auto-save** hote thakbe (38s interval)

2b Content lekho — rich editor diye

Editor body click kore lekha shuru koro. WYSIWYG mane "jeta dekhcho seita-i publish hobe" — Word/Google Docs er moto behave kore.

Toolbar theke chaile bold, italic, heading, list, quote, code block, link, image embed — sob niye nite parbe.

💡 Shortcut keys

Ctrl+B bold · Ctrl+I italic · Ctrl+K link · / insert menu

2c Featured image diye sundor banao

Right sidebar-e "Featured Image" card-e click koro. Media Library kholbe — already-uploaded image select koro, ba notun ekTa drag & drop kore upload koro.

Ei image-i Facebook/LinkedIn share korle preview-e dekhabe, blog list page-e thumbnail hobe.

⚠️ Image size guideline

Optimal: 1200×630px · max 1 MB · JPG ba PNG. CMS auto-WebP convert kore CDN-e push korbe.

Categories & tags — content gucchiye rakho

Sidebar e Content → Categories & Tags click koro. Eikhane content-er taxonomy manage korbe.

Social media schedule — manually post korar din shesh

Sidebar e Social → Content Scheduler click koro. Calendar view-e dekhabe ekhon obdi koto post planned ache, kon channel e, kokhon.

3 way te social post hoy:

Way 1 Cross-post from blog

Blog editor er sidebar e tick kore (Step 2e)

Way 2 Standalone social post

Scheduler-e "+ Schedule post" → image/video/text + channels select

Way 3 Bulk import (CSV)

Excel-e 30 din-er content plan → upload → all queued

Facebook: 10 AM & 7 PM · LinkedIn: 12 PM weekday · Instagram: 6-8 PM. CMS engagement data theke auto-suggest kore.

Step 7

Team add koro — role assign koro

Sidebar e People → Users → + Invite user. Email + role select koro — invitation auto-send hobe. User accept korle 2FA setup korbe, then access pabe.

Super Admin

Highest

Sob kichu: billing, security keys, delete org. Shudhu tomar ar co-founder/CTO er jonno.

Admin

High

Day-to-day management: users invite, content publish, social manage. Cannot touch billing.

Editor

Medium

Content team: create, edit, publish posts. Upload media. Schedule social. Cannot delete users or change settings.

Viewer

Low

Read-only access: dashboard, analytics, reports dekhte parbe. Kichu change korte parbe na. Stakeholder/client er jonno valo.

🎯 Recommended workflow

Daily 15-minute routine

Just 15 minutes a day diye tumi ekTa dynamic, growing CMS chalate parbe.

🌅 Morning · 5 min

Check-in

  • 📊 Dashboard er KPI 4 Ta dekho
  • 🔔 Notifications check koro (FB comments, mentions)
  • ✓ Today's scheduled posts thik thak chalu kina

🌞 Midday · 5 min

Engage

  • 💬 Engagement inbox e new comments e reply koro
  • 👍 LinkedIn reposts e thank-you bolo
  • 📷 IG stories e tagged content repost koro

🌙 Evening · 5 min

Plan tomorrow

  • 📅 Scheduler-e tomorrow er post review koro
  • ✏️ Quick draft start koro — Rich editor
  • 🚀 Schedule publish for next morning

📅 Weekly · 30 min (Monday)

Strategic review

  • 📈 Analytics: top 3 performing posts ki?
  • 📉 Worst 3 posts — keno work korlo na?
  • 🎯 Next week-e 3-5 Ta post idea jot koro
  • 📊 Reports module theke email export — stakeholders ke send koro

🗓️ Monthly · 1 hour

Maintenance

  • 🛡️ Audit log review — koi action thik na
  • 🔑 Social tokens expire korche kina check
  • 🗑️ Trashed posts clean koro
  • 👥 Team member access review
  • 📊 SEO score < 70 posts re-optimize

Tumi ekhon ready! 🚀

Practice korar best way — admin kholo, ekTa test post likho, draft kore rakho. CMS-e kichu break korbe na — tumi safely experiment korte paro.
