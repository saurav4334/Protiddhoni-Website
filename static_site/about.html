Our story

Why Protiddhoni Was Created

Bangladesh has experienced rapid digital growth, yet voice communication remains one of the most effective ways to reach people across every district, city, and community.

Many businesses still struggle with customer engagement because traditional communication channels often go unread, ignored, or require internet access. Protiddhoni was created to bridge that gap.

Our goal is simple: enable businesses, organizations, healthcare providers, educational institutions, and service providers to communicate with people through reliable, scalable voice technology.

By combining automation, APIs, and intelligent call workflows, we help organizations reach thousands of people with a single campaign while maintaining a personal customer experience.
